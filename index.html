BEHIND THE NUMBERS

The card in this game

The fictional card has a $1,000 limit, a 24% APR and a minimum payment of $35 or the full statement balance if it’s lower. You never spend real money.

At each statement, the game subtracts your payment, then adds 2% interest (24% ÷ 12) to the remaining balance. Paying in full adds no interest in this model. Real issuers often calculate interest daily; grace periods, minimums, fees and trailing interest vary by card. The game leaves out fees and credit scores.
